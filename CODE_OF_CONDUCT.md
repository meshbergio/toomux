# Code of Conduct

Contributions and technical disagreement are welcome. Participation here
requires treating other people with respect.

Do not use harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language,
sexualised attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption or publication of
another person's private information. Critique code, designs and evidence
rather than the person presenting them.

Maintainers may edit or remove material, lock discussions, or restrict
participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
Good-faith reports and technical disagreement are not misconduct merely
because they are critical.

For a conduct issue involving sensitive information, use GitHub's private
reporting/moderation mechanisms where available rather than posting the details
publicly.
